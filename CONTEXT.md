# CPAP Mask Evidence Experience

This context describes how patient-reported body-site evidence is presented and explored on CPAP mask pages.

## Language

**Heatmap Region**:
A visible area of the face, head, or neck that represents one or more body sites in the comfort heatmap.
_Avoid_: Hotspot, body site

**Summary Widget**:
A compact callout outside the face that points to a Heatmap Region and summarizes its evidence.
_Avoid_: Tooltip, label

**Expanded Evidence View**:
The in-depth panel for one selected Heatmap Region, including evidence types, associated mask components, and representative review excerpts.
_Avoid_: Tooltip, modal

**Detailed View**:
A heatmap mode that displays Summary Widgets for every sufficiently supported Heatmap Region at once.
_Avoid_: Expanded view, evidence panel

**Test Cohort**:
The ten masks shown on the temporary heatmap page: AirFit P10, AirFit F20, AirFit N20, ComfortGel Blue Nasal, Swift FX, Quattro FX, Nuance Pro, AirFit F40, Swift FX Bella For Her, and SleepWeaver Advance.
_Avoid_: Sample data, top ten masks
