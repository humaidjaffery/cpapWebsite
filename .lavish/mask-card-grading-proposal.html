Aspect grades use one vote per distinct review. Every grade will show its evidence count on hover/detail; fewer than five reviews displays “—”.

Six stats are the right catalog-level set

Fit, Comfort, Seal, Sleep Compatibility, Ease of Use, and Durability cover the purchase decision while staying readable in a three-column catalog. Noise, airflow, skin, value, and therapy results remain on the detail page.

Keep the owner rating visible

The letter at the top is based only on the original 1–5 ratings. The exact average and sample size sit next to it, so the grade never hides the underlying evidence.

Best match must be behavioral or physical

Prioritize sleep position, movement, facial hair, face size, mouth breathing, skin sensitivity, pressure, and claustrophobia. Do not lead with generic groups such as “long-term user.”

No evidence is a valid result

A mask with fewer than five supported reviews for a stat receives “—”, not a guessed grade. Five to nineteen reviews are marked provisional; 20–99 moderate; 100+ strong.

Recommended calculation

Remove sentiment magnitude; count review outcomes

Overall review grade

Use only the original numeric owner rating. Convert the 1–5 range to a 0–100 position so one star is the bottom and five stars is the top:

(average stars − 1) ÷ 4 × 100

The F20’s 4.33 average becomes 83.25, or B+. No extracted text sentiment enters this grade.

Fit, comfort, seal and other grades

Reviews do not provide numerical aspect ratings. Use extracted sentiment only as a coarse direction, never as a magnitude:

  • 6–10 → positive report
  • 5 → neutral/mixed report
  • 0–4 → negative report

Merge all claims within one review into one vote, then calculate (positive + ½ mixed) ÷ reviews.
